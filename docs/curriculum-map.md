# Curriculum Map — team guide

The **Curriculum Map** is the living wall that shows how the LACE curriculum is organized: legal skills in columns of notes, substantive law as a tile grid, and room to rearrange as the program evolves.

It lives in **Brightspace Manager** under **Author → Curriculum Map**.

## What you can do

1. Open the page and browse the current map (no edit mode needed just to look).
2. Click **Edit map** to make changes:
   - Rename branches, columns, and notes
   - Add or remove notes and tiles
   - Drag items to reorder
   - Mark notes as topic vs. sub-topic, or flag tentative items
3. Click **Save** when you want the team to see your changes.
4. Click **Done** (or **Cancel**) to leave edit mode. **Done** does not undo a successful save.

**Last save wins.** If two people edit at the same time, whoever saves last updates the shared map. There is no merge or conflict screen — coordinate on bigger restructuring if needed.

## Who can edit

You need to be **signed in** to Brightspace Manager (same Google sign-in as the rest of the app). Saves are tied to your email in the background for accountability.

## Where changes are stored

There is **one shared map** for the whole team. When you save, the full map is stored in our Supabase database — not in Brightspace courses and not on your laptop. Everyone sees the same version after a refresh.

If the page shows an amber **“Showing mock data”** banner, the app could not load the live map (usually a one-time setup issue). Edits in that state will not stick until live data is connected. Ask a platform admin if you see that banner.

## Tips

- **Save before you walk away** — edit mode is only on your screen until you save.
- **Small, frequent saves** are safer than one huge edit session.
- Use **comments** on notes for private margin thoughts; they are for planners, not learners.
- The map is for **planning and alignment**. Building actual course content still happens in Course Studio and Brightspace.

## Quick troubleshooting

| What you see                      | What to try                                                                                |
| --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Save fails or “permission denied” | Refresh, sign out and back in, or contact a platform admin.                                |
| Amber mock-data banner            | Live map is not loading — do not rely on saves until an admin fixes the connection.        |
| Your changes disappeared          | You may have clicked **Cancel**, refreshed before saving, or someone else saved after you. |
